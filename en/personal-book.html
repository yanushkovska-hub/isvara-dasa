IInside

What’s inside

No two of these books share a table of contents. The chapters grow out of your chart and your questions. No children – no chapters about children. If your main question is about your calling, a move or your family line, a whole large part grows around it.

The book begins with where you draw your strength from:

Totem of personal strength

Your natural source of energy. You can return to it when you feel empty and weighed down.

Spiritual name

In the Nama-karana tradition it is revealed by the sound of the lunar mansion you were born under. There is no need to change your passport: this name is carried in silence, for yourself.

These first pages move people the most.

Then comes your whole life, area by area. There is a chapter on the body: where your reserves of strength are, where the weak spots are and what is worth having checked regularly by doctors. It is a guide for prevention; only a doctor makes diagnoses.

The book comes with an e-volume, “900 Questions Worth Asking an Astrologer”, answered for your chart.

IIIAccuracy

Why there are no templates in the book

My own calculation software

I wrote it myself: precise Swiss Ephemeris, the Hipparcos satellite star catalogue, 49 systems of planetary periods. Everything is calculated on my own computer – your data goes nowhere.

Cross-checked across schools

The foundation is classical Parashara Jyotish, alongside KP, Jaimini, Nadi and Lal Kitab. Only what two or three systems point to at once goes into the book.

Checked against your life

Using 10–20 events from your biography, I refine your birth time and find the periods that actually work in your life.

Every book from scratch

The software compares the text with every previous book: eight identical words in a row, and the work stops.

IVHow it works

How it works

  1. 1
    Introduction on Zoom. We talk about your life and what you want to understand.
  2. 2
    Data. Date, exact time and place of birth. 10–20 dated life events: moves, jobs, marriages, children, illnesses, losses. If your spouse, children or parents are part of the book – their data too.
  3. 3
    Your questions. As many as you like: 100, 200, 500. Let them repeat and contradict each other – that is normal. Everything that can be answered from the chart, I answer in the chapters.
  4. 4
    My questionnaire. I ask my own questions so that the book speaks about your real life.
  5. 5
    Calculation. I check your birth time against events and calculate the chart.
  6. 6
    Writing. Three to five months. The clock starts on the day all the data and answers are in.
  7. 7
    Layout, printing, delivery. I send the finished book by post to any country.

Corrections: I fix factual errors – for example, if the date of an event was given incorrectly. I don’t rewrite the conclusions of the chart to suit wishes.

VIIFAQ

Questions and answers

I don’t know my exact birth time. Will you take it on?

If the range is 10–15 minutes, I will first check the time against events in your life and then tell you. If the time is completely unknown, I don’t write the book: without an exact time, the calculation turns into guesswork.

How long does the book take?

Three to five months from the day all the data, questions and questionnaire answers are in. I send the finished book by post to any country.

Can I get the book as a PDF?

No. The book exists in a single printed copy – yours.

Will there be anything hard to read?

Every chart has difficult places, and a book without them would be dishonest. I put them in a separate appendix and explain how to live with them. I don’t predict death.

What if I don’t like a conclusion?

I will correct a factual error. I don’t rewrite the conclusions of the chart to suit wishes.

What happens to my data?

Calculations run on my own computer; nothing goes to the cloud. I don’t publish people’s stories or name names.

Can I ask questions after the book?

Yes, in a separate consultation: you choose the topics you want to explore more deeply.
