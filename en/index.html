To put it simply: I bring into one picture what a person had scattered across different corners of their life. I don’t hand out ready-made instructions – I give a lens through which a person then sees the answers for themselves.

Even at my first consultations I noticed that a conversation lasts 2.5 hours, yet people feel that only 5 minutes have passed. They went into trance on their own. Later I went deeper into classical hypnosis and combined it with Ericksonian hypnosis – since 2022 it has been my regular practice.

Īśvara Dāsa is my spiritual name; I received it at initiation in the Nama-japa tradition. I never had another profession “before this”: this is my life.

There are no photos of me on this site. It is a personal rule connected with my chart. My books speak about me better.
