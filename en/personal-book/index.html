Questions and answers

I don’t know my exact time of birth. Will you take it on?

If the uncertainty is 10–15 minutes, I first check the time against events in your life and then let you know. If the time is completely unknown, I don’t write the book: without an exact time the calculation turns into guesswork.

Will there be anything hard to read in the book?

Every chart has difficult places, and a book without them would be dishonest. They are gathered in a separate, gentle appendix: no scare tactics or fatalism, and with guidance on how to handle them. I do not predict death.

Can I get the book as a PDF?

No. The book exists in a single printed copy, and it is yours. Only the “900 Questions” volume comes in electronic form.

Can I give the book as a gift to someone close to me?

Yes, if you have their exact birth data and the dates of events in their life.

What happens to my data?

All calculations are done offline on the author’s computer; nothing goes to the cloud. I don’t publish people’s stories or mention names.
