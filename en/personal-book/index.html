  • The body’s weak spots according to the chart, the years when a check-up matters most and the daily habits that support you. It is a guide for prevention: astrology does not diagnose or treat.

  • Cities and countries where you breathe easier and grow faster, and places where your strength drains away. Useful when you choose where to live, buy a home or start a business.

  • The great periods of your life, the coming years and an annual chart for each year, with a calendar at the end. You see in advance when it is time for a leap and when for rest and protection, and you plan your steps calmly.

  • The most vulnerable places in the chart: where strength and money leak away and what throws you off balance. It is all gathered in a separate, gentle appendix, with no scare tactics or fatalism, together with advice on how to handle it. I do not predict death.

  • Practical actions for your chart: for the main planets, for difficult places and for specific goals. When to do them, on which days and at what hours, and which mantras to say aloud. You can begin right after reading.

  • Will, clarity, action and change: four powers you rely on. You will see which of them leads you, which is worth strengthening and how together they form your path.

Questions and answers

What is a personal Jyotish book?

A personal Jyotish book is a 200–500 page book that Īśvara Dāsa writes himself for one person, based on their Vedic astrology birth chart. It has 12–15 chapters on areas of life: character, purpose, work and money, love and marriage, children, family lineage, health, place to live and a forecast by year. It is printed in a single hardcover copy.

How is the book different from a horoscope or an astrology consultation?

A sun-sign horoscope is the same for everyone born in the same month. A one-hour consultation covers one or two questions and is soon forgotten. The book describes one person from their exact chart, answers all their questions — 10, 50 or 100 — and stays at hand for years.

How long does it take to write the book?

One to three months from the moment you share your data and pay for the book. No more than five books are in progress at a time, so places each season are limited.

What do I need to order the book?

Your date, exact time and place of birth, 10–20 life events with dates, and your questions. The first step is a free 20-minute introductory Zoom call, where the author tells you the price and whether he can take on your chart.

How does the introduction go?

It is a 20-minute conversation on Zoom with no obligations. You tell me what you want to understand, I clarify your birth data and tell you the price. After the conversation you calmly decide whether to begin.

How much does the personal book cost?

The price is fixed: one all-inclusive package with no short or demo versions. It includes the printed book, chapters on your spouse, children and parents, the “900 Questions” e-book, delivery to any country and a final Zoom meeting. The author tells you the price personally at the free introductory call.

I don’t know my exact time of birth. Will you take it on?

If the uncertainty is 10–15 minutes, I first check the time against events in your life and then let you know. If the time is completely unknown, I don’t write the book: without an exact time the calculation turns into guesswork.

Will there be anything hard to read in the book?

Every chart has difficult places, and a book without them would be dishonest. They are gathered in a separate, gentle appendix: no scare tactics or fatalism, and with guidance on how to handle them. I do not predict death.

Can I get the book as a PDF?

No. The book exists in a single printed copy, and it is yours. Only the “900 Questions” volume comes in electronic form.

Can I give the book as a gift to someone close to me?

Yes, if you have their exact birth data and the dates of events in their life.

What happens to my data?

All calculations are done offline on my computer; nothing goes to the cloud. I show owners’ stories and names only with their consent.
